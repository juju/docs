This document describes details specific to using your existing Amazon EC2 cloud with Juju. 

> See more: [Amazon EC2](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ec2/?icmpid=docs_homepage_featuredsvcs) 

When using the Amazon EC2 cloud with Juju, it is important to keep in mind that it is a (1) [machine cloud](/t/5454#heading--machine-clouds-vs--kubernetes-clouds) and (2) [not some other cloud](/t/5454#heading--cloud-foo-vs--cloud-bar). 

> See more: [Cloud differences in Juju](/t/5454#heading--cloud-differences)

As the differences related to (1) are already documented generically in our [Tutorial](/t/6559), [How-to guides](/t/5334), and [Reference](/t/5348) docs, here we record just those that follow from (2).
