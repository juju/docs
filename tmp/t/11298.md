In a Juju [deployment](/t/11285), a **worker** is a process that a Juju [agent](/t/5466) runs in the background on a Juju entity ([controller](/t/5455), [model](/t/5456), [machine](/5459/), [unit](/t/5460), etc.) and which has responsibilities related to that entity.
